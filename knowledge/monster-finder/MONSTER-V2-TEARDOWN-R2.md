But v2 is NOT build-ready. Round-2's fresh lenses expose a consistent failure mode the math-gate rewrite did not touch: every NEW input/navigation surface (text/voice/AI/hop-rail) and every persisted store inherits ZERO of the hardening that was lavished on the word-grid path. Net: the foundation is solid and most fixes are mechanical, but the NEW-surface debounce/identity/a11y holes and the infeasible proof harness are must-fix gates before step 1.
